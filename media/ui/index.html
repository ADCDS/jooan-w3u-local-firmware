        Day and night

        Both lenses share the infrared filter and lights.
        Darkness now
        day belownight above
        The level is the pan/tilt lens’s exposure: higher is darker. With the filter open it reads about 3.6× lower at twilight, so keep the day level below the night level ÷ 3.6.

        Infrared LEDs
        Off: night mode without the infrared LEDs, in black and white, for a street with its own lights. The filter still switches.

        Noise reduction: 0 leaves grain, 255 is very strong and may smear moving things.

        Both lenses. Match your mains frequency (60 Hz in Brazil) to stop banding under LED street lights and indoor lights.

        Audio

        The camera’s microphone and speaker.

        Audio is off on the camera (audio=0), so there is no microphone or talk.

        Microphone

        Sent with both main video streams as 8 kHz G.711 (A-law).

        Speaker

        Used for talk and the test tone.
        Volume and gain last until a restart; the microphone switch is kept. Volume 60 is unity; a step is about 0.5 dB.

        Talk through the camera

        Uses this browser’s microphone. The browser asks for permission the first time.
